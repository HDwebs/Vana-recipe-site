The Cook Up

Serves 1
570
Kcal
50g
Protein
42g
Carbs
9g
Fat
Breakfast High Protein Eggs
Ingredients
  • 3 turkey bacon rashers
  • 2 chicken chipolatas
  • 1 egg
  • 1 slice of bread
  • 100g mushroom
  • 100g beans
Method
  1. Place sausages in the grill or oven.
  2. After 5 minutes, add the bacon.
  3. Chop the mushrooms, spray a pan with a couple of 1kcal fry sprays, add the mushrooms then the egg (or poach the egg).
  4. Heat the beans in a small pan.
  5. Toast the bread to your preference.
  6. Plate and enjoy.
