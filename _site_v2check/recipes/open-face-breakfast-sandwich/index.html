Open Face Breakfast Sandwich

Serves 1
194
Kcal
11g
Protein
15g
Carbs
9g
Fat
Breakfast Eggs
Ingredients
  • 1/2 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 clove garlic, minced
  • 10g sun-dried tomatoes (not packed in oil), chopped
  • 1 large egg
  • 10g low-fat feta cheese
  • Coarse rock salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 slice wholegrain bread, toasted
  • Fresh chopped parsley (optional)
Method
  1. In a large non-stick pan, heat the oil over medium-low heat.
  2. Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes and cook, stirring, for 1-3 minutes or until the garlic is fragrant and translucent.
  3. Crack the egg into the pan and sprinkle with feta, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cover and cook undisturbed until the egg is cooked to your liking.
  5. Jiggling the pan slightly will allow you to check the yolk - a runny yolk will move, a fully cooked yolk will be firm.
  6. Transfer the egg along with some of the tomato and garlic to the toast and sprinkle with parsley if desired.
