Sirloin Steak Salad

Serves 2
391
Kcal
62g
Protein
0g
Carbs
17g
Fat
Dinner High ProteinLow CarbZero Carb BeefSalad
Ingredients
  • 2 x 200g sirloin steaks, 2-3cm thick
  • 1 tbsp sunflower oil
  • Sea salt
  • Black pepper
Method
  1. Take the steaks out of the fridge 60 minutes before cooking. Pat dry and season both sides with sea salt.
  2. Heat a large frying pan over very high heat until hot. Add the oil, then immediately place the steaks in. Turn every minute for an even brown crust. Cook for approx. 3 minutes total for rare, 5 for medium, 8 for well done. Use a thermometer for accuracy: 50°C rare, 60°C medium, 70°C well done. If the steak has a fat cap, hold it on its side with tongs to render it golden.
  3. Plate, cover with foil and rest for 5 minutes. Serve with a salad of your choice.
