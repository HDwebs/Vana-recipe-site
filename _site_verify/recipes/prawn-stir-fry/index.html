Prawn Stir Fry

Serves 2
652
Kcal
73g
Protein
105g
Carbs
11g
Fat
Dinner High Protein SeafoodAsian
Ingredients
  • 2 carrots, sliced thin lengthways
  • 130g baby corn
  • 100g tenderstem broccoli
  • 100g courgettes
  • 1 large red pepper
  • 1/2 a medium white cabbage
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • 1cm piece fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 200g cooked prawns
  • 200g noodles of choice
Method
  1. Finely slice all the veg into roughly equal-sized pieces. Heat the oil in a large wok and fry the garlic and ginger for 60 seconds.
  2. Add the veg to the wok and toss to coat. Fry for 2-3 minutes, then pour over the soy sauce and mix well. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the veg starts to soften.
  3. Add the prawns, stir and heat through. Serve over the cooked noodles.
