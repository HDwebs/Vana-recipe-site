Breakfast Muffin

Serves 1
481
Kcal
30g
Protein
26g
Carbs
29g
Fat
Breakfast High Protein FakeawayEggs
Ingredients
  • 1 breakfast muffin
  • 2 lean bacon rashers
  • 1 pork sausage
  • 1 egg
Method
  1. Cook the sausage for 5 minutes, then add the bacon.
  2. Cook the egg - for that classic round shape, cook it in a mini pancake mould.
  3. Lightly toast the muffin.
  4. Slice the sausage lengthways.
  5. Add your favourite sauce and assemble.
