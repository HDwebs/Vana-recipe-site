Pulled Chicken in Slow Roasted Tomatoes

Serves 1
400
Kcal
47g
Protein
13g
Carbs
17g
Fat
Dinner High ProteinLow Carb Chicken
Ingredients
  • 1 large beef tomato, diced
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 15g olive oil
  • 1 large chicken breast (180-220g)
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried mint
  • 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 generous tsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • A pinch of flaky sea salt
Method
  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. In a roasting dish, add the diced tomato with the onion, olive oil, a pinch of salt and pepper, balsamic, lemon and herbs, and mix together well.
  3. Place the chicken breast on top of the tomatoes in the tray and add another pinch of salt.
  4. Roast for 30-40 minutes or until the sauce starts to thicken and the chicken is cooked through.
  5. Remove from the oven and, using two forks, shred the chicken and mix it into the sauce.
