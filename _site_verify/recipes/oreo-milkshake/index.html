Oreo Milkshake

Serves 1

Ingredients
  • 2 scoops protein powder
  • 2 Oreos
  • 1 banana
  • 250ml almond milk
Method
  1. Place all ingredients into a blender.
  2. Blend.
  3. Enjoy.
Top Tip: Reduce 89 kcals by removing the banana if wanted.
