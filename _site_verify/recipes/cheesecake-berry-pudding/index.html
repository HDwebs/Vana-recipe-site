Cheesecake Berry Pudding

444 kcal serves 1, or split into 2 portions of 222 kcal each
444
Kcal
44g
Protein
24g
Carbs
20g
Fat
Dessert High Protein
Ingredients
  • 250g ricotta
  • 1 scoop of flavoured whey protein powder
  • 150g berries of choice
  • 1 tsp lemon juice (optional)
  • Sweetener (optional)
Method
  1. Place the protein powder and ricotta into a bowl and mix well until combined.
  2. Add the berries (or any fresh fruit) with a small amount of lemon juice and stir to combine - if they break up slightly it adds to the swirl effect.
