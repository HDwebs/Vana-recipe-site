ingredients:
  - "2½ tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 tuna steaks (about 140g each)"
  - "½ cucumber"
  - "2 spring onions, finely chopped"
  - "1 large tomato, finely chopped"
  - "1 small red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped"
  - "Handful of chopped parsley"
  - "1 lime or lemon, juiced"
method:
  - "Put the oil in a food bag with the tuna steaks, rub well and leave for 30 minutes while you make the relish. Peel and halve the cucumber lengthways, remove the seeds with a spoon, then chop into small squares. Combine with the remaining relish ingredients, squeeze in the lime or lemon juice, mix and season to taste."
  - "Heat a griddle pan until very hot, add the steaks and cook 2 minutes per side (tuna is best served a little pink). Rest for up to 5 minutes off the heat, then top with the relish and serve."
