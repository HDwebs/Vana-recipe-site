ingredients:
  - "100g rice (nutrition based on brown rice)"
  - "150g frozen peas"
  - "50g French beans"
  - "2 spring onions, sliced"
  - "½ tsp dried chilli flakes"
  - "Handful of coriander, diced"
  - "2 tsp tamari"
  - "1 tbsp sesame oil"
  - "1 tbsp vegetable oil"
  - "2 large eggs"
  - "2 large cod loins (280g), cut into thick strips"
method:
  - "Cook the rice in a pan of water for about 25 minutes, adding the peas and beans for the last 6 minutes. Drain, return to the pan and stir in the spring onions, chilli flakes, tamari, sesame oil and most of the coriander (save a tablespoon for the eggs). Cover."
  - "Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan. Beat the eggs with the reserved coriander. Dip the cod strips in the egg mixture, then fry 2 minutes until golden brown. Remove the cod, add the rice to the pan with any remaining egg, and stir for about 90 seconds. Serve the rice in bowls topped with the fish."
