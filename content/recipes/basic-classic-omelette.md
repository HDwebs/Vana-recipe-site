ingredients:
  - "3 large eggs"
  - "1 tsp butter"
  - "1 tsp sunflower oil"
  - "Fillers of your choice (mushrooms, ham, onions etc.) — not included in nutrition"
method:
  - "Beat the eggs well and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Heat the oil and butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat until the butter has fully melted and is frothing."
  - "Add the eggs, swirling the pan to cover the full surface. Cook 15–20 seconds, then scrape around the edge with a spatula."
  - "Tilt the pan to let any runny egg fill the gaps. Repeat until there's no more runny egg."
  - "Add your chosen fillers, fold the omelette gently in half, cook a few more seconds, then serve."
tip: "Fillers are not included in the nutrition values above."
