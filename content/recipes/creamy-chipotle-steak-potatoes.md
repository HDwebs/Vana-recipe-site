ingredients:
  - "For the steak bites: 800g (28oz) raw topside beef, cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - "1.5 tsp salt"
  - "1.5 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1.5 tsp dried parsley"
  - "1 tsp cumin"
  - "1.5 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "Juice of 1 lime"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "For the glaze: 20g (1oz) reduced fat butter"
  - "1.5 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "For the potatoes: 800g (28oz) potatoes, cut into small cubes"
  - "2 tsp Italian herbs"
  - "1.5 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1.5 tsp paprika"
  - "1.5 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp olive oil (optional)"
  - "For the chipotle sauce: 15g (1 tbsp) garlic, chopped"
  - "100g (3.5oz) red onion, chopped"
  - "1 tsp Italian herbs"
  - "50g (1.8oz) chipotle paste"
  - "250ml (9oz) light evaporated milk (or any milk)"
  - "100g (3.5oz) 50% fat cream cheese"
  - "30g (1.1oz) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated"
  - "1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped"
method:
  - "Toss the steak cubes with the salt, garlic powder, dried parsley, cumin, smoked paprika, lime juice and olive oil until well coated."
  - "Toss the potato cubes with the Italian herbs, garlic powder, paprika, salt and olive oil."
  - "Spread the potatoes on a lined tray and oven bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 20–22 minutes until golden and crispy."
  - "Meanwhile, melt 5g of butter in a pan over medium-high heat and sear half the steak bites for 2–3 minutes per side until a golden crust forms."
  - "Take the pan off the heat, add another 5g of butter and half the fresh parsley, and toss to coat. Set aside and repeat with the second batch."
  - "In the same pan over medium-low heat, sauté the garlic and red onion for 5 minutes until soft. Stir in the Italian herbs and chipotle paste for 2–3 minutes, then add the evaporated milk, cream cheese and parmesan and stir until smooth and creamy."
  - "Add the crispy potatoes to the sauce and coat every piece. Divide into 4 portions with the steak bites and finish with the remaining parsley."
tip: "Marinate the steak for at least 10–15 minutes and don't overcrowd the pan. The sauce doesn't need cooking down — just stir it over low heat until smooth, then add the potatoes."
