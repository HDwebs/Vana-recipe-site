ingredients:
  - "For the beef: 1kg (35oz) raw 5% fat beef mince"
  - "Avocado oil cooking spray"
  - "250g (8.8oz) white onion, chopped"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "1 tsp black pepper"
  - "1.5 tsp paprika"
  - "1 tsp cumin"
  - "20g (1oz) minced garlic"
  - "60g (2.1oz) tomato paste"
  - "Extra seasoning: ½ tsp each salt and pepper, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin (plus extra tomato paste if needed)"
  - "For the pasta: 260g (9.1oz) uncooked pasta of choice"
  - "For the garlic mint yoghurt: 400–500g (14–17.6oz) fat free Skyr"
  - "1.5 tsp minced garlic"
  - "1 tsp dried mint"
  - "Pinch of salt"
  - "Juice of ½ lemon"
  - "For the chilli butter: 40–50g (1.4–1.8oz) light butter"
  - "½ tsp paprika"
  - "Pinch of salt"
  - "½ tsp chilli flakes (to taste)"
  - "15g (0.5oz) tomato paste"
  - "A splash of reserved pasta water"
  - "To garnish: chopped cherry tomatoes, fresh mint and chilli garlic seasoning (optional)"
method:
  - "Make the garlic mint yoghurt: stir the Skyr, garlic, mint, salt and lemon juice together until smooth. Refrigerate until needed."
  - "Cook the pasta in boiling water for about 8 minutes until al dente. Reserve some pasta water, then drain and set aside."
  - "Spray a pan with avocado oil and sauté the onion over medium heat for 8–10 minutes until soft and golden. Add the salt, pepper, paprika, cumin, garlic and tomato paste and cook for 3–4 minutes until fragrant."
  - "Turn the heat to high, add the beef mince and break it up. Cook for about 8 minutes until browned and cooked through, then add the extra seasoning to taste."
  - "Make the chilli butter: melt the butter in a small pan over low heat, add the paprika, chilli flakes, tomato paste, salt and a splash of pasta water, and stir until smooth and glossy."
  - "Divide the pasta and beef into 5 portions. Top each with a few spoonfuls of the garlic mint yoghurt and a drizzle of chilli butter, then garnish with cherry tomatoes, mint and chilli garlic seasoning."
tip: "Take your time getting the onions golden before the spices and tomato paste go in. Store the mint yoghurt separately so it stays fresh through the week."
