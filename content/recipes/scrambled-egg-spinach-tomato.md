ingredients:
  - "6 baby plum tomatoes, halved"
  - "4 large eggs"
  - "2 tbsp natural yogurt (or dairy-free alternative)"
  - "175g spinach, washed and dried well"
  - "1 tbsp rapeseed oil"
  - "Black pepper, to taste"
method:
  - "Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the tomatoes cut-side down with the spinach. While they cook, whisk the eggs and yogurt with black pepper."
  - "Move the tomatoes and spinach to serving plates. Add a little more oil to the pan if needed, pour in the eggs and stir occasionally until scrambled to your liking. Serve over the spinach."
