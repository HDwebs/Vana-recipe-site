ingredients:
  - "3 large sweet potatoes"
  - "100g baby spinach"
  - "6 medium-large eggs"
  - "100g feta, broken into bits"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
method:
  - "Prick the potatoes and microwave until soft, about 6–8 minutes, then let cool."
  - "Heat the oil in a deep ovenproof frying pan and wilt the spinach. Halve the sweet potatoes lengthways and scoop out the flesh in big chunks. Whisk the eggs."
  - "Add the sweet potato to the pan and stir in the spinach, keeping it lumpy rather than flattened. Pour in the eggs, swirling into any gaps. Scatter over the broken feta and cook over low heat 4–5 minutes until the sides and bottom are set."
  - "Finish under a grill for 1–2 minutes to set the top. Cool slightly before slicing to serve. Keeps chilled for up to a day."
