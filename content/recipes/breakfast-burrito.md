ingredients:
  - "1 tortilla wrap"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "125g 2% turkey mince"
  - "50g salsa"
  - "1 cheese slice"
  - "50g onion"
  - "Paprika"
  - "Cumin"
  - "Salt"
  - "Coriander"
method:
  - "Whisk the eggs with paprika and salt, set aside."
  - "Cook the turkey with the rest of the seasoning, adjusting spice to your desired heat level. Once cooked, place the turkey in a dish."
  - "Use the same pan to scramble the egg, then place on a plate when done."
  - "Place the wrap on a plate and add the salsa, turkey, egg and cheese. Wrap them compactly."
  - "Using the cleaned pan, lightly spray with oil and on medium heat cook the wrap seam-side down until golden brown, then flip and cook the other side the same way."
  - "Enjoy."
