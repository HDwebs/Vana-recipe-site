ingredients:
  - "For the steak bites: 800g (28oz) raw topside beef, cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - "1.5 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp black pepper"
  - "2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "2 tsp dried parsley"
  - "1 tsp chilli powder"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "For the garlic butter glaze: 40g (1.5oz) reduced fat butter"
  - "30g (2 tbsp) minced garlic"
  - "2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "For the Cajun spice mix: 1 tsp each salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, chilli powder (or cayenne), thyme and oregano"
  - "For the mac n cheese: 200g (7oz) uncooked macaroni"
  - "280ml (10oz) 1% milk"
  - "150g (5.3oz) 50% fat cream cheese"
  - "35g (1.3oz) light cheddar"
  - "35g (1.3oz) grated mozzarella"
method:
  - "Season the steak cubes with the salt, pepper, garlic powder, dried parsley, chilli powder and olive oil. Leave to marinate for 10 minutes."
  - "Cook the macaroni in boiling water for about 8 minutes until al dente, then drain and set aside."
  - "Meanwhile, melt 10g of butter in a large pan over medium-high heat and sear half the steak bites for about 3 minutes per side until golden brown."
  - "Turn the heat to low, add another 10g of butter with half the garlic and fresh parsley, and toss to coat. Remove and keep warm. Repeat with the second batch."
  - "In the same pan on low heat, add the milk, cream cheese, cheddar, mozzarella and Cajun spice mix. Stir continuously until smooth and creamy."
  - "Stir the pasta through the sauce, garnish with parsley and serve with the steak bites, divided into 4 portions."
tip: "Don't overcrowd the pan — sear in batches for a proper crust. If the sauce gets too thick once the cheese melts, loosen it with a splash of milk or pasta water."
