ingredients:
  - "For the bulgogi beef: 900g (32oz) raw topside beef, cut into thin strips"
  - "30g (1.1oz) light soy sauce"
  - "20g (0.7oz) dark soy sauce"
  - "2 tsp black pepper"
  - "1.5 tsp chilli flakes"
  - "1.5 tsp chilli powder"
  - "1 tbsp minced garlic"
  - "1 tbsp ginger paste"
  - "1 tbsp sesame seeds"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "20g (1oz) reduced fat butter, for cooking"
  - "For the kimchi fried rice: 210g (7.4oz) uncooked jasmine rice (≈560g cooked)"
  - "15g (1 tbsp) garlic, chopped"
  - "20g (1.5 tbsp) gochujang paste"
  - "80g (2.8oz) spring onion, whites chopped"
  - "150g (5.3oz) carrot, grated"
  - "100g (3.5oz) kimchi, chopped"
  - "30g (2 tbsp) kimchi juice"
  - "50g (1.8oz) spring onion, greens chopped"
  - "30g (1.1oz) soy sauce"
  - "To garnish: sesame seeds"
method:
  - "Cook the rice ahead of time and let it cool."
  - "Marinate the beef strips with the light and dark soy, black pepper, chilli flakes, chilli powder, garlic, ginger, sesame seeds and olive oil. Set aside while you prep everything else."
  - "Heat a little butter in a pan over medium-high heat and sear the beef in batches for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and slightly crispy. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan over medium heat, add a little more butter if needed, then stir-fry the garlic, gochujang, spring onion whites and carrot for 2–3 minutes until fragrant."
  - "Add the cooked rice and stir well so every grain is coated. Cook for 2–3 minutes until it turns a vibrant orange and is heated through."
  - "Add the kimchi, kimchi juice, spring onion greens and soy sauce and cook for another 2–3 minutes."
  - "Return the beef to the pan, mix through and heat for 1–2 minutes. Garnish with sesame seeds and divide into 4 portions."
tip: "Marinate the beef for at least an hour, or overnight, for deeper flavour. Day-old rice gives the best fried-rice texture, but freshly cooked and cooled rice works too."
