ingredients:
  - "2 x 200g sirloin steaks, 2-3cm thick"
  - "1 tbsp sunflower oil"
  - "Sea salt"
  - "Black pepper"
method:
  - "Take the steaks out of the fridge 60 minutes before cooking. Pat dry and season both sides with sea salt."
  - "Heat a large frying pan over very high heat until hot. Add the oil, then immediately place the steaks in. Turn every minute for an even brown crust. Cook for approx. 3 minutes total for rare, 5 for medium, 8 for well done. Use a thermometer for accuracy: 50°C rare, 60°C medium, 70°C well done. If the steak has a fat cap, hold it on its side with tongs to render it golden."
  - "Plate, cover with foil and rest for 5 minutes. Serve with a salad of your choice."
