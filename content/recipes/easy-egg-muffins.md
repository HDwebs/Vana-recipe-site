ingredients:
  - "1 tbsp oil"
  - "150g broccoli, chopped"
  - "1 red pepper, chopped"
  - "1 large spring onion, sliced"
  - "6 medium eggs"
  - "1 tbsp milk"
  - "Large pinch of smoked paprika"
  - "50g cheddar cheese, grated"
  - "Small amount of chives, for topping"
method:
  - "Heat the oven to 200°C/180°C fan. Oil an 8-hole muffin tin with half the oil. Heat the rest in a pan and fry the broccoli, pepper and onion for 5 minutes, then set aside to cool."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, smoked paprika and 25g of the cheese in a large bowl. Stir in the broccoli, pepper and onion mix. Pour into the muffin tray holes, top each with the remaining cheese and chives, and bake 15–18 minutes until golden brown. Serve."
