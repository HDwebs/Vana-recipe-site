ingredients:
  - "2 salmon fillets (220g)"
  - "1 tbsp sunflower oil, for greasing"
  - "For the glaze: 2 tsp brown miso paste"
  - "2 tsp balsamic vinegar"
  - "2 tsp soy sauce"
  - "1 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "90g rice of choice (nutrition based on jasmine)"
method:
  - "Heat the grill to high. Mix the miso paste, balsamic vinegar, soy sauce and paprika to make the glaze, then brush over the salmon fillets."
  - "Place the salmon skin-side down on a greased baking tray and grill 6–8 minutes until cooked."
  - "Cook the rice, plate up and top with the grilled miso salmon."
