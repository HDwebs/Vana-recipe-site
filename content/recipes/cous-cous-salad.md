ingredients:
  - "100g couscous"
  - "200ml hot vegetable stock"
  - "1 spring onion"
  - "1 red pepper"
  - "1/2 cucumber"
  - "50g feta cheese, crumbled"
  - "2 tbsp pesto"
  - "2 tbsp pine nuts (or nuts of choice)"
method:
  - "Pour the hot vegetable stock over the couscous, cover and leave for 10 minutes until all the stock is absorbed and the couscous is fluffy."
  - "Slice the spring onion, pepper and cucumber and add to the couscous along with the pesto and crumbled feta. Scatter the pine nuts over to serve."
