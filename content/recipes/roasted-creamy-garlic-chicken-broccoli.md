ingredients:
  - "For the chicken: 800g (28oz) raw chicken breast, butterflied"
  - "2.5 tsp oregano"
  - "2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1 tsp chilli flakes"
  - "2 tsp paprika"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "For the broccoli: 600g (21oz) broccoli, cut into small florets"
  - "1.5 tsp chilli flakes"
  - "1.5 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "1.5 tsp black pepper"
  - "40g (1.4oz) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated"
  - "100g (3.5oz) red onion, thinly sliced"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "5 whole garlic cloves (roasted for the sauce)"
  - "For the white sauce: 100g (3.5oz) low fat yoghurt"
  - "80g (2.8oz) 50% fat mayo"
  - "80g (2.8oz) light feta"
  - "20g (1oz) honey"
  - "Juice of ¼ lemon"
  - "To garnish: a handful of walnuts, crushed"
method:
  - "Butterfly the chicken breasts and season both sides with the oregano, garlic powder, chilli flakes, paprika, salt and olive oil. Set aside to marinate."
  - "Toss the broccoli florets with the chilli flakes, garlic powder, salt, pepper, parmesan, red onion and olive oil."
  - "Spread on a tray with the 5 whole garlic cloves in the centre and oven bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 15–16 minutes."
  - "Meanwhile, cook the chicken in a pan over medium heat for 5–6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Rest, then cut into cubes."
  - "Squeeze the roasted garlic from the tray into a blender with the yoghurt, mayo, crumbled feta, honey and lemon juice. Blend until completely smooth."
  - "Layer the broccoli on a serving dish, top with the chicken and drizzle with the white sauce. Mix gently, garnish with crushed walnuts and divide into 4 portions."
tip: "Rest the chicken before cubing to keep it juicy, and don't let the broccoli catch — burnt broccoli turns bitter. Stores well cold, or reheat in the air fryer at 180°C for 5 minutes."
