ingredients:
  - "60g block halloumi, cut into 4 slices"
  - "2 large slices fresh pineapple"
  - "Little Gem lettuce"
  - "Burger buns of choice (thin buns used)"
  - "½ red cabbage, sliced"
  - "2 carrots, grated"
  - "1 large radish, sliced"
  - "Handful of coriander, diced"
  - "Juice of 2 limes"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Pinch of chilli flakes"
  - "1 tbsp chipotle paste"
method:
  - "Combine the sliced cabbage, grated carrot, sliced radish and coriander in a bowl. Add the lime juice, ½ tbsp olive oil and a pinch of chilli flakes, season to taste, and mix well by hand. Can be made ahead and stored in the fridge."
  - "Mix the remaining ½ tbsp oil with the chipotle paste and coat the halloumi slices. Griddle the halloumi and pineapple together for 4 minutes, turning halfway, until the cheese is golden and the pineapple starts to caramelise. Lightly toast the inside of the burger buns."
  - "Build the burgers with lettuce, slaw, halloumi and pineapple. Serve with any remaining slaw and lettuce on the side."
