ingredients:
  - "For the steak bites: 900g (32oz) raw topside beef, cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - "2 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp black pepper"
  - "1.5 tsp dried parsley"
  - "2 tsp oregano"
  - "2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1.5 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "For the chimichurri glaze: 30g (1.1oz) reduced fat butter"
  - "30g (2 tbsp) minced garlic"
  - "2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "1 tsp chilli flakes"
  - "Juice of ½ lemon"
  - "Flaky salt, to garnish"
  - "For the cheesy mash: 850g (31oz) white potatoes, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks"
  - "150ml (5.3oz) light evaporated milk"
  - "100g (3.5oz) grated mozzarella"
  - "1 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "1 tsp black pepper"
method:
  - "Toss the steak cubes with the salt, pepper, dried parsley, oregano, garlic powder, smoked paprika and olive oil until evenly coated."
  - "Boil the potatoes for 12–14 minutes until fork-tender. Drain, then return to the pan with the lid on for 5 minutes to steam off excess moisture."
  - "Mash until smooth, then stir in the evaporated milk, mozzarella, garlic powder, salt and pepper until creamy."
  - "Heat a little of the butter in a pan over medium-high heat and sear the steak bites for about 2 minutes per side until a golden, charred crust forms."
  - "Turn the heat to low and add the remaining butter, garlic, fresh parsley, chilli flakes and lemon juice. Stir until the beef is coated in the chimichurri butter."
  - "Divide the mash and steak bites into 4 portions and finish with flaky salt."
tip: "Season topside generously — it's a great lean cut but needs the flavour. To reheat, warm everything in a pot on low for 5 minutes, adding a splash of milk if the mash is dry."
