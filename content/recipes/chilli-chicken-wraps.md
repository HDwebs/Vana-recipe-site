ingredients:
  - "4 medium wraps"
  - "2 large skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces"
  - "2 tbsp vegetable oil"
  - "1 large onion, thinly sliced into half-moons"
  - "2 garlic cloves, finely chopped"
  - "Small piece of ginger, peeled and finely chopped"
  - "1 tbsp tomato purée"
  - "1 red chilli, thinly sliced"
  - "Juice of ½ lemon"
  - "1 small red onion, sliced"
  - "2 tbsp mango chutney"
  - "2 handfuls coriander"
  - "50ml natural yogurt"
method:
  - "Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Brown the chicken, then remove. Add the sliced onion, ginger, garlic and a pinch of salt, and cook 4–6 minutes until softened."
  - "Turn the heat to high, return the chicken to the pan, add the tomato purée, chilli and lemon juice. Season well and cook until the chicken is tender, about 10 more minutes."
  - "Warm and plate the wraps, add the chicken, then top with red onion, mango chutney, coriander and yogurt. Roll up and serve."
