ingredients:
  - "For the beef: 800g (28oz) raw 5% fat beef mince"
  - "20g (0.7oz) butter, for cooking"
  - "20g (1.5 tbsp) minced garlic"
  - "100g (3.5oz) white onion, chopped"
  - "75g (2.7oz) red pepper, chopped"
  - "75g (2.7oz) green pepper, chopped"
  - "75g (2.7oz) yellow pepper, chopped"
  - "1.5 tsp each salt, black pepper, paprika and chilli flakes"
  - "For the sauce: 140g (5oz) 50% fat cream cheese"
  - "50g (1.8oz) grated mozzarella"
  - "3–4 light cheese slices (19g each)"
  - "125ml (½ cup) reserved pasta water"
  - "For the pasta: 240g (8.5oz) uncooked macaroni"
  - "To garnish: fresh parsley, chopped"
method:
  - "Cook the macaroni in boiling water for 7–8 minutes until just slightly underdone. Reserve a cup of pasta water, then drain and set aside."
  - "Meanwhile, melt the butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the garlic, onion and peppers and sauté for 5–6 minutes until soft."
  - "Push the vegetables to the edges of the pan, add the beef with the salt, pepper, paprika and chilli flakes, and break it up. Cook on high heat for 6–8 minutes until fully browned, then mix through the vegetables."
  - "Turn the heat to low and add the cream cheese, mozzarella, cheese slices and 125ml of the reserved pasta water. Stir continuously until the cheese melts into a smooth sauce."
  - "Stir in the macaroni, adding a splash more pasta water if needed. Divide into 4 portions and garnish with parsley."
tip: "Lower the heat before adding any dairy to stop the sauce curdling, and make sure the beef is fully cooked before the cheese goes in."
