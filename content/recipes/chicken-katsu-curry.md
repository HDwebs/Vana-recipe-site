ingredients:
  - "For the sauce: 1 tbsp vegetable oil"
  - "2 medium onions, diced"
  - "1 large carrot, sliced"
  - "2 garlic cloves, crushed"
  - "Small piece of ginger, finely chopped"
  - "1 tbsp curry powder"
  - "½ tsp ground turmeric"
  - "1 tin reduced-fat coconut milk (about 400ml)"
  - "2 tsp honey"
  - "For the katsu: 1 tbsp cornflour"
  - "2 chicken breasts, sliced"
  - "100g breadcrumbs"
  - "Also needed: vegetable oil for frying"
  - "1 pouch (250g) cooked rice of choice"
  - "½ cucumber, peeled into ribbons"
  - "Coriander leaves"
  - "Lime wedges"
method:
  - "Make the sauce: heat 1 tbsp oil in a frying pan, cook the onions and carrots until soft and starting to caramelise. Add the garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds, then stir in the curry powder and turmeric. Add the coconut milk, honey and 100ml water. Season and simmer, lid on, over low heat for 20 minutes."
  - "Prepare the katsu: mix the cornflour with 4 tbsp water and seasoning in a bowl. Dip the chicken strips in the mixture, then coat in breadcrumbs."
  - "Blend the sauce until smooth, thinning with a little water if needed. Taste and adjust seasoning. Keep warm."
  - "Heat oil in a frying pan and cook the chicken 4–6 minutes each side until golden and cooked through. Warm the rice and divide between bowls. Top with the curry sauce and breaded chicken, and serve with cucumber ribbons, coriander and lime wedges."
tip: "To adapt for vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free diets, swap the chicken for tofu and use gluten-free breadcrumbs and tamari in place of standard soy sauce/breadcrumbs."
