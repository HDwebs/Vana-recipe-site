ingredients:
  - "For the fries: 900g (32oz) raw potatoes, cut into fries"
  - "1.5 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp oregano"
  - "2 tsp onion powder"
  - "2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "2 tsp paprika"
  - "1 tsp cumin"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "For the garlic sauce: 100g (3.5oz) low fat yoghurt"
  - "80g (2.8oz) 50% fat mayo"
  - "½ tsp each salt, garlic powder and onion powder"
  - "Juice of ½ lime"
  - "For the taco beef: 800g (28oz) raw 5% fat beef mince"
  - "100g (3.5oz) white onion, chopped"
  - "30g (1.1oz) reduced fat butter, for cooking"
  - "70g (2.5oz) low fat cheddar or mozzarella"
  - "For the taco spice mix: 1 tsp each salt, black pepper, paprika, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder and cumin"
  - "To garnish: chopped red onion, tomatoes, coriander and jalapeños, plus a squeeze of lime"
method:
  - "Slice the potatoes into fries and toss with the salt, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, cumin and olive oil until evenly coated. Oven bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for about 20 minutes until golden and crispy. Set aside."
  - "Make the garlic sauce: mix the yoghurt, mayo, salt, garlic powder, onion powder and lime juice until smooth and creamy. Set aside."
  - "Heat a pan over medium-high heat, add the butter and chopped onion and sauté for about 8 minutes until soft and caramelised. Remove and set aside."
  - "In the same pan, cook the beef mince over medium-high heat for 6 minutes until browned. Add the taco spice mix and the caramelised onions and cook for another 4–5 minutes until well combined and deepened in colour."
  - "Lower the heat, sprinkle over the cheese and cover the pan for a minute or two until it melts."
  - "Divide the fries into 4 portions, top with the cheesy taco beef, red onion, jalapeños, tomatoes and coriander, then drizzle with the garlic sauce."
tip: "Toss the fries thoroughly so the seasoning coats evenly. Make the garlic sauce in a bigger batch and keep it in the fridge — it works with plenty of other meals too."
