ingredients:
  - "3 garlic cloves"
  - "2 tbsp rapeseed oil"
  - "1 small onion, sliced"
  - "1 tsp sweet smoked paprika"
  - "1 tin cannellini beans, drained and rinsed"
  - "1 tin chopped tomatoes with herbs"
  - "1 tbsp brown sauce"
  - "6 large mushrooms, halved"
  - "2 thyme sprigs"
  - "400g cherry tomatoes"
  - "6 small sausages of choice (recipe uses meat-free sausages)"
  - "4 small eggs"
method:
  - "Preheat the oven to 200°C/180°C fan. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a small saucepan, fry the onion 8–10 minutes until soft and golden. Add the crushed garlic and paprika, cook 50–60 seconds, then add the beans, tomatoes and brown sauce and bring to the boil. Reduce to a simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, then cover to keep warm."
  - "Put the thyme, cherry tomatoes and mushrooms in a roasting tray with the remaining garlic and oil. Roast 12 minutes until soft, then cover to keep warm. Grill the sausages on full heat for 5–6 minutes each side until golden and cooked through."
  - "Bring a pan of lightly salted water to the boil, swirl to create a whirlpool, then poach each egg for 3 minutes and remove with a slotted spoon. Serve the roasted veg with the sausages, eggs and beans, seasoning to taste."
