ingredients:
  - "For the steak bites: 800g (28oz) raw topside beef, cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - "15g (1 tbsp) minced garlic"
  - "2 tsp Himalayan salt"
  - "2 tsp black pepper"
  - "2 tsp mixed herbs (parsley, oregano, thyme, rosemary)"
  - "1 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "1 tsp olive oil"
  - "20g (1oz) reduced fat butter, for cooking"
  - "For the potatoes: 800g (28oz) potatoes, cut into small cubes"
  - "2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "2 tsp mixed herbs"
  - "2 tsp paprika"
  - "1 tsp olive oil"
  - "For the sauce: 100g (3.5oz) white onion, finely chopped"
  - "100g (3.5oz) chestnut mushrooms, finely chopped"
  - "100g (3.5oz) cherry tomatoes, chopped"
  - "250ml (8.8oz) 1% milk"
  - "140g (5oz) 50% fat cream cheese"
  - "40g (1.4oz) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated"
  - "Juice of ½ lemon"
  - "To finish: 30g (1.1oz) light cheddar, chilli flakes and fresh parsley"
method:
  - "Toss the steak cubes with the garlic, salt, pepper, mixed herbs, smoked paprika and olive oil until evenly coated. Set aside."
  - "Toss the potato cubes with the garlic powder, mixed herbs, paprika and olive oil. Spread on a lined tray and bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 20–23 minutes until golden and crispy."
  - "While the potatoes cook, melt some of the butter in a pan over medium-high heat and sear the steak bites in batches, about 3 minutes per side, until a golden crust forms. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan over medium heat, cook the onion, mushrooms and cherry tomatoes for 5 minutes until soft. Lower the heat and stir in the milk, cream cheese and parmesan for 3–4 minutes until smooth, thick and creamy."
  - "Add the lemon juice, then return the steak bites and crispy potatoes to the pan and stir to coat. Top with the cheddar and chilli flakes, garnish with parsley and divide into 4 portions."
tip: "Marinate the steak for at least 30 minutes, or overnight, for deeper flavour. Keep the heat low when the milk and cream cheese go in so the sauce doesn't split. Freezes well for weeks."
