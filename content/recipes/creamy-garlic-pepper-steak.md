ingredients:
  - "For the steak: 800g (28oz) raw topside beef, cut into thin strips"
  - "2 tsp salt"
  - "2 tsp black pepper"
  - "2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "2 tsp paprika"
  - "1.5 tsp chilli flakes"
  - "2 tsp olive oil"
  - "25g (1oz) reduced fat butter, for cooking"
  - "For the sauce: 280ml (10oz) 1% milk"
  - "150–180g (5.3–6.4oz) 50% fat cream cheese"
  - "40g (1.4oz) parmesan, grated"
  - "½ tsp each salt and black pepper"
  - "1 tsp garlic powder"
  - "A big handful of spinach"
  - "For the rice: 180g (6.3oz) uncooked white rice (≈540g cooked, 135g per serving)"
  - "Mixed herbs, salt and chilli flakes, to season"
method:
  - "Season the steak strips with the salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, chilli flakes and olive oil. Marinate for at least 15 minutes."
  - "Bring the rice and water to the boil, then simmer for 8–10 minutes until cooked. Season with mixed herbs, salt and chilli flakes and set aside."
  - "Heat the butter in a pan over medium-high heat and cook the steak for 5–6 minutes until golden brown, without overcrowding the pan."
  - "Lower the heat to medium-low and add the milk, cream cheese, parmesan, salt, pepper and garlic powder. Stir until smooth and creamy."
  - "Stir in the spinach and simmer for 5–8 minutes until the sauce thickens and the spinach wilts."
  - "Divide into 4 portions and serve with the seasoned rice."
tip: "Slice the steak thinly to keep it tender, and lower the heat before the milk and cheese go in so the sauce doesn't split."
