  - "½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil"
method:
  - "Halve the avocado, remove the stone and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Squeeze in the lemon juice and mash with a fork to your desired texture. Season with sea salt, black pepper and chilli flakes."
  - "Toast the bread, drizzle with olive oil, then pile the smashed avocado on top."
