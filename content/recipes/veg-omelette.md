ingredients:
  - "Egg white (e.g. Two Chicks) — 10 tbsp"
  - "Whole egg (e.g. Burford Brown, Clarence Court) — 1"
  - "Mushrooms — 50g"
  - "Red bell pepper — 50g"
  - "Feta cheese — 50g"
method:
  - "Whisk the egg white and whole egg together in a bowl."
  - "Slice the mushrooms and red pepper, and dice the feta."
  - "Heat a non-stick pan and sauté the mushrooms and pepper until softened."
  - "Pour in the whisked egg and cook over medium heat."
  - "Scatter the feta over one half, fold the omelette and cook until set."
  - "Serve immediately."
