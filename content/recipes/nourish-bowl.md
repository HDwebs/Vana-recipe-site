  - "Thai jasmine rice (e.g. Veetee) — 140g"
  - "Pine nuts, raw — 10g"
  - "Walnuts — 10g"
  - "Cucumber — 100g"
  - "Rocket — 100g"
  - "Garlic infused olive oil (e.g. Waitrose) — 15ml"
  - "Tenderstem broccoli (e.g. Sainsbury's) — 100g"
method:
  - "Cook the jasmine rice according to pack instructions."
  - "Season and grill or pan-fry the chicken breast until cooked through, then slice."
  - "Steam or blanch the tenderstem broccoli until just tender."
  - "Slice the cucumber."
  - "Build the bowl: rice as the base, top with chicken, broccoli, cucumber and rocket."
  - "Scatter over the pine nuts and walnuts."
  - "Drizzle with the garlic infused olive oil and serve."
tip: "This is a build-your-own bowl. Swap the protein (chicken, boiled/fried egg, wild caught salmon), the grain (basmati or jasmine rice), the toppings (almonds, pine nuts, pomegranate seeds, sunflower seeds, walnuts), the veg (cucumber, green leaves, kale, spring onion, tomato, or sweet potato, carrot, broccoli, spinach, avocado) and the dressing (apple cider vinegar, garlic, chilli infused olive oil, mustard, lemon or lime juice, tahini, sugar-free mayonnaise) to keep it fresh week to week."
