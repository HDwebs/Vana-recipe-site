ingredients:
  - "1/2 tablespoon olive oil"
  - "1/2 clove garlic, minced"
  - "10g sun-dried tomatoes (not packed in oil), chopped"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "10g low-fat feta cheese"
  - "Coarse rock salt and freshly ground black pepper"
  - "1 slice wholegrain bread, toasted"
  - "Fresh chopped parsley (optional)"
method:
  - "In a large non-stick pan, heat the oil over medium-low heat."
  - "Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes and cook, stirring, for 1-3 minutes or until the garlic is fragrant and translucent."
  - "Crack the egg into the pan and sprinkle with feta, salt, and pepper."
  - "Cover and cook undisturbed until the egg is cooked to your liking."
  - "Jiggling the pan slightly will allow you to check the yolk - a runny yolk will move, a fully cooked yolk will be firm."
  - "Transfer the egg along with some of the tomato and garlic to the toast and sprinkle with parsley if desired."
