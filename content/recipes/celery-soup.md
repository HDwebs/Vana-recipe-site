ingredients:
  - "300g celery, sliced (tough strings removed)"
  - "1 garlic clove, peeled"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "200g potatoes, peeled and sliced"
  - "500ml vegetable stock"
  - "100ml skimmed milk"
  - "Bread of choice, to serve (not included in nutrition)"
method:
  - "Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the celery, garlic and potatoes, stir to coat. Add a splash of water and a pinch of salt. Stir regularly for about 15 minutes."
  - "Add the stock, bring to the boil, then reduce heat and simmer for about 20 minutes until the potatoes are breaking apart and the celery has softened."
  - "Blend with a hand blender until smooth, then stir in the milk and blend further. Season to taste and serve with bread of your choice."
tip: "Bread is not included in the nutrition values above."
