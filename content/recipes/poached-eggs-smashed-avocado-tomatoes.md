ingredients:
  - "2 tomatoes, halved"
  - "½ tbsp rapeseed oil"
  - "Juice of 1 lime"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "1 ripe avocado"
  - "2 slices wholegrain bread"
  - "Large handful of rocket"
method:
  - "Heat a frying pan with a dash of rapeseed oil and cook the tomatoes cut-side down until soft and browning. Meanwhile bring a pan of water to a gentle simmer, carefully add the eggs and poach 1–2 minutes until the whites are firm but yolks still runny."
  - "Remove the stone from the avocado, scoop the flesh into a bowl, add the lime juice and mash. Spread onto the bread, top with the poached eggs, black pepper and a handful of rocket. Serve the tomatoes on the side."
