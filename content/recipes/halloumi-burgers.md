ingredients:
  - "125g block halloumi cheese, cut into 4 thick slices"
  - "½ tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 burger buns (thin buns used)"
  - "2 tbsp hummus"
  - "1 large ripe tomato, sliced"
  - "Large lettuce leaves"
  - "Fresh tomato salsa (pre-bought)"
method:
  - "Light the BBQ (or use a griddle pan). Brush both sides of the halloumi slices with olive oil and cook until golden, about 3 minutes per side."
  - "Toast the insides of the buns. Spread hummus on the base bun, add tomato, the golden halloumi, lettuce and salsa. Top with the other bun, secure with a skewer stick and serve."
