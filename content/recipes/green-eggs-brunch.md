ingredients:
  - "300g spinach"
  - "2 medium eggs"
  - "2 leeks, trimmed and sliced"
  - "2 garlic cloves, sliced"
  - "½ tsp coriander seeds"
  - "½ tsp fennel seeds"
  - "Pinch of chilli flakes"
  - "2 tbsp Greek yogurt"
  - "Lemon, to squeeze"
  - "1½ tbsp olive oil"
method:
  - "Heat the oil in a frying pan, add the leeks with a pinch of salt and cook until soft, about 4 minutes. Add the fennel and coriander seeds, garlic and chilli flakes. Once the seeds start crackling, add the spinach, reduce the heat, and mix until the spinach reduces. Push to one side of the pan, add a little more oil, then fry the eggs to your preference."
  - "Mix the yogurt into the spinach and season to taste. Split between two plates, top with the fried eggs, a squeeze of lemon and a finish of black pepper and chilli flakes."
