ingredients:
  - "125ml oat milk"
  - "2 eggs, separated"
  - "1 banana"
  - "100g rolled oats"
  - "2 tsp baking powder"
  - "Flavoured extract of choice (vanilla recommended)"
  - "Oil spray, for the pan"
method:
  - "Blend the oats, oat milk, egg yolks, banana, baking powder and extract until smooth. Whisk the egg whites until stiff, then fold 1–2 tbsp of the whites into the batter."
  - "Heat a large non-stick pan over medium heat and spray lightly with oil. Add small amounts of batter and cook 1–2 minutes until the base sets and bubbles appear, then flip and cook the other side for a minute. Repeat, plating up with your topping of choice."
