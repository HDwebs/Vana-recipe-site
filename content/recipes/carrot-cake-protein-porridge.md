ingredients:
  - "50g oats"
  - "1/2 carrot, finely grated"
  - "20g raisins"
  - "150ml almond milk"
  - "1 scoop vanilla protein powder"
  - "1 tsp cinnamon"
  - "Walnuts, to top"
method:
  - "Place the almond milk in a saucepan on low heat, add the oats, cinnamon and carrot."
  - "Stir continually until the oats are cooked, adding more liquid if needed."
  - "Once cooked, remove from the heat and stir in the protein powder."
  - "Stir in the raisins and transfer to a bowl."
  - "Top with walnuts and enjoy."
