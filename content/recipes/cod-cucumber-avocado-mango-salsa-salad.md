ingredients:
  - "2 skinless cod fillets"
  - "1 small mango, peeled and chopped"
  - "1 small avocado, peeled and sliced"
  - "¼ cucumber, sliced"
  - "160g cherry tomatoes, chopped"
  - "1 red chilli, deseeded and chopped"
  - "2 spring onions, sliced"
  - "1 lime"
  - "Handful of chopped coriander"
method:
  - "Heat the oven to 200°C/180°C. Place the cod in an ovenproof dish, pour over half the lime juice, add a little zest and black pepper. Cook 8 minutes until the fish flakes but stays moist."
  - "While the cod cooks, combine the mango, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, chilli, spring onion, avocado and coriander in a bowl. Add the remaining lime juice and zest, mix well. Spoon onto plates, top with the cod and any juices from the dish."
