Breakfast Burrito

Serves 1
535
Kcal
55g
Protein
39g
Carbs
18g
Fat
Breakfast High Protein Mexican
Ingredients
  • 1 tortilla wrap
  • 2 eggs
  • 125g 2% turkey mince
  • 50g salsa
  • 1 cheese slice
  • 50g onion
  • Paprika
  • Cumin
  • Salt
  • Coriander
Method
  1. Whisk the eggs with paprika and salt, set aside.
  2. Cook the turkey with the rest of the seasoning, adjusting spice to your desired heat level. Once cooked, place the turkey in a dish.
  3. Use the same pan to scramble the egg, then place on a plate when done.
  4. Place the wrap on a plate and add the salsa, turkey, egg and cheese. Wrap them compactly.
  5. Using the cleaned pan, lightly spray with oil and on medium heat cook the wrap seam-side down until golden brown, then flip and cook the other side the same way.
  6. Enjoy.
