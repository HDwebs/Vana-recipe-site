Carrot Cake Protein Porridge

Serves 1
456
Kcal
29g
Protein
49g
Carbs
13g
Fat
Breakfast High Protein
Ingredients
  • 50g oats
  • 1/2 carrot, finely grated
  • 20g raisins
  • 150ml almond milk
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Walnuts, to top
Method
  1. Place the almond milk in a saucepan on low heat, add the oats, cinnamon and carrot.
  2. Stir continually until the oats are cooked, adding more liquid if needed.
  3. Once cooked, remove from the heat and stir in the protein powder.
  4. Stir in the raisins and transfer to a bowl.
  5. Top with walnuts and enjoy.
