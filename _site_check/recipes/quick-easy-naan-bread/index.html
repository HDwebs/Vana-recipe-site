Quick & Easy Naan Bread

Makes 5 naans
265

6g
Protein
42g
Carbs
8g
Fat
Side BreadIndian
Ingredients
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 120ml milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, plus extra for greasing
Method
  1. For the dough, sift the flour, sugar, salt and baking powder into a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, mix together the milk and oil. Make a well in the centre of the flour mixture and pour in the liquid, slowly mixing from the centre outwards to make a smooth, soft dough.
  3. Knead well for 8-10 minutes, adding a little flour if the dough is too sticky.
  4. Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp tea towel and leave in a warm place for 10-15 minutes.
  5. Form the dough into five balls.
  6. Preheat the grill to medium and place a heavy baking sheet on the upper shelf to heat.
  7. Roll the dough balls out thinly and pull into a teardrop shape (not essential, but makes them look authentic). Sprinkle over your chosen topping and press into the surface.
  8. Place the naans onto the hot baking sheet and grill for 1-2 minutes, or until lightly browned.
  9. Brush with butter and serve hot.
