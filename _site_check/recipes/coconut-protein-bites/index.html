Coconut Protein Bites

Makes 12 balls — macros shown are per ball
99
Kcal
6g
Protein
5g
Carbs
6g
Fat
Snack
Ingredients
  • 100g almond butter
  • 45g whey protein powder
  • 40g coconut flour
  • 40g honey
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla flavouring
  • 65ml skimmed milk
  • Desiccated coconut for dressing
Method
  1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl except the desiccated coconut.
  2. Weigh out the mixture and divide into 12 equal bites, rolling each one in the palms of your hand.
  3. Roll them in the coconut.
  4. Place the balls on a plate or in an airtight container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
